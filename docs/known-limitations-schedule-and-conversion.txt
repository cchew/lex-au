Known limitations: schedule rendering and DOCX-to-AKN conversion
===============================================================

Companion to docs/known-limitations-empty-body.txt. Each entry is a standing,
accepted limitation, not a regression the spot-check or fidelity audit should
keep re-flagging -- except where an entry itself says "Resolved", which means
the underlying pipeline defect is fixed and the entry is kept only as a
historical record.


(a) Two Acts retain pre-v0.9.0 XML (stale _resolve_title apostrophe fallback)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Slugs:
  industrial-relations-court-(judges'-remuneration)-act-1993
  royal-australian-air-force-veterans'-residences-act-1953

The v0.9.0 `--force` re-ingest did not re-convert these two Acts. `_resolve_title`
has an apostrophe-handling fallback that fails to match the canonical
legislation.gov.au title for names containing a typographic apostrophe, so the
fetch step resolved no compilation and the build was skipped. Their committed XML
is therefore the pre-v0.9.0 output: no schedule tables, older reflinks placement.
Fixing `_resolve_title` needs its own patch (tracked separately); until then a
targeted re-convert of these two Acts is blocked.

Live probe 2026-09-04 (api.prod.legislation.gov.au/v1), and DESCOPED from v0.9.1.
The v0.9.1 design (unit A1) proposed a glyph-swap `name eq` retry -- replace the
U+0027 apostrophe with U+2019 and re-query -- on the premise that the stored
title uses a typographic apostrophe. The probe disproves that premise:

  - Both Acts exist and are isInForce == true:
      C2004A04659  Industrial Relations Court (Judges' Remuneration) Act 1993
                   (year 1993, number 104)
      C1953A00092  Royal Australian Air Force Veterans' Residences Act 1953
                   (year 1953, number 92)
  - The stored `name` uses U+0027 (straight ASCII apostrophe), not U+2019:
    `contains(name,'Judges' Remuneration')` / `contains(name,'Veterans' Residences')`
    with a curly apostrophe return 0 rows; the same `contains()` on an
    apostrophe-free fragment ("Remuneration) Act 1993", "Residences Act 1953",
    "Industrial Relations Court (Judges") returns the record.
  - `name eq '<straight apostrophe>'` returns HTTP 400 -- an OData
    string-literal syntax error at the apostrophe position -- with or without
    the `and isInForce eq true` clause, and for the RAAF Act, which has no
    parenthesis at all. The break is the bare unescaped U+0027, not an
    "apostrophe-then-parenthesis" interaction.
  - `name eq '<curly apostrophe>'` (the proposed glyph swap) returns HTTP 200
    but an empty result set: the stored name is straight, so the swapped query
    matches nothing.

So the glyph-swap `eq` retry is inert for these two Acts, and the existing
`contains()` fallback still fails because every trimmed fragment it builds
retains the U+0027 and re-triggers the 400. A working fix must neutralise the
apostrophe inside the `contains()` fragment (or select an apostrophe-free
distinctive fragment) and confirm with a glyph-insensitive exact match -- larger
than the v0.9.1 branch table allows. Deferred to a future patch. The 2 Acts
(0.065% of the corpus) stay on pre-v0.9.0 XML, as disclosed above.


(b) Prose after a schedule <paragraph>/<subparagraph> can attach above it
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
**Resolved in v0.10.0** (SDD plan 2026-09-08-wfw-v0.3.1-phase2-4, Task 2).
`_build_schedule_content` (src/lexau/builder.py) previously reset the running
`current_content` pointer to None after a clause boundary, a subclause boundary
and a TABLE element, but NOT after the PARAGRAPH or SUBPARAGRAPH branches, so
loose BODY prose following a schedule <paragraph>/<subparagraph> attached to the
<content> opened before it instead of a fresh one after it -- rendering above the
<paragraph> in document order instead of after it.

Fixed by resetting `current_content = None` at the end of both branches too,
matching the existing TABLE-reset pattern. Regression-tested
(tests/test_builder.py, schedule-content module) and live in the full v0.10.0
corpus re-convert. Was low-incidence pre-existing behaviour (schedules rarely
interleave numbered paragraphs with trailing loose prose); no longer occurs.


(c) ParseReport.tables_found counts body tables only
---------------------------------------------------
`ParseReport.tables_found` is incremented only on the body-path TABLE handler.
Schedule tables, rendered by `_build_schedule_content` since v0.9.0, are not
counted. A ParseReport showing `tables_found: 0` for an Act that in fact has
schedule rate/tariff/repeal tables is expected, not a conversion loss. The audit
(`scripts/audit_conversion_fidelity.py`) measures rendered <td>/<th> cells
directly and is the authoritative count for schedule-table content.


(d) v0.9.0 corpus XML predates the _content_for parent-affinity fix
------------------------------------------------------------------
The C1 fix in `builder.py` `_build_schedule_content._content_for` (guard:
`current_content.getparent() is not parent`) landed AFTER the v0.9.0 `--force`
re-ingest. The committed v0.9.0 corpus XML was therefore built with the pre-fix
builder: in the affected Acts, schedule `Note:`/`Penalty:` lines and short block
headings sit under a clause-level <content> where they should be subclause-level
(or schedule-level), or vice versa. Only the cite-eId of those notes/headings is
wrong; no operative subclause or paragraph text is affected and no text is lost
or duplicated (`prose_set_same` held on every affected Act).

Full incidence (enumerated 2026-09-04 by `scripts/detect_c1_schedule_reattach.py`
over all 1,429 `hcontainer name="subclause"` Acts): 45 Acts affected, 1,383
clean, `corporations-act-2001` not measured (detector's 90s per-Act cap;
re-converted anyway, see below). Every affected Act had 1-48 schedule <p>
re-parented with `prose_set_same=True`. Largest: national-security-legislation-
amendment-(espionage-and-foreign-interference)-act-2018 (48),
anti-terrorism-act-(no.-2)-2005 (35), defence-service-homes-act-1918 (27).

Resolved: all 45 affected Acts have been re-converted on disk against the fixed
builder -- a targeted `lexau build --acts "<name>" --force` per Act. The 25
highest-incidence Acts, plus `corporations-act-2001`, were re-converted during
the v0.9.0 fix wave; the remaining 20 (1-2 re-parented <p> each) were
re-converted for `v0.9.1`. No compilation changed on any Act (`comp_num` before
== after); `corporations-act-2001` schedule <td> count is 17127 before and after
(C1 only re-parents note/heading <p>, never touches table cells). For every
re-converted Act the on-disk schedule <content>/<p> placement now matches the
fixed HEAD builder and differs from the pre-fix (a5ad3c2) builder in the
expected clause <-> subclause direction for the affected Note/Penalty/Example
and short-heading lines. corpus/index.json and the on-disk XML carry the
corrected eIds; the v0.9.1 export pushed them to the HF dataset.

`scripts/detect_c1_schedule_reattach.py` enumerates the affected set by diffing
the pre-fix (a5ad3c2) and HEAD `_build_attachments` output per Act; it does not
read the on-disk corpus XML, so it still reports all 45 as "affected" -- that is
its purpose (identify which Acts need the fixed builder), not a remediation
gate. Re-conversion is confirmed by rebuilding and diffing the saved XML against
the fixed builder.

The 1-line guard is committed and regression-tested
(tests/test_builder.py::test_schedule_prose_after_note_stays_under_subclause),
so every future build is correct.


(e) DOCX figure extraction: static-site gap, quotedStructure edge
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOCX `<figure>` images are extracted to `corpus/images/` as of v0.9.1.

**The vector backlog is resolved as of v0.10.0** (SDD plan
2026-09-08-wfw-v0.3.1-phase2-4, Task 18B). The 59-Act backlog noted in earlier
versions of this entry was found to actually be 97 Acts / 1,512 missing images
at full-corpus scale, caused by a `_convert_chunk` batching defect: a single
non-zero exit / timeout / OSError discarded the ENTIRE soffice-conversion
chunk of up to 8 images, even when most of that chunk's images had already
converted successfully. Fixed: `_convert_chunk` now finalises every image
that actually landed on disk regardless of how the batch call ended, and all
97 affected Acts (including 5 ITAA-scale giants whose source DOCX runs
8.8-27.9 MB) have been re-swept. `corpus/images/` now holds 2,084 files; 0
Acts have any missing-image placeholder, verified by a direct `<img src>` vs
on-disk file-existence scan across the full 3,084-Act corpus.

`lexau site`, the static-site generator, does not copy `corpus/images/`, so
figures are unresolved in static-site output. The lex-au-explorer reader
consumes the images directly and resolves them by basename.

A `<figure>` with resolvable image blobs nested inside a `<quotedStructure>`
sub-builder currently restarts figure numbering at `-fig-1` and can point at
the parent Act's `-fig-1` image. 0 corpus cases today; the spec design (§D10)
scoped this out.

(v0.9.2) 15 orphan duplicate `-fig-N<letter>` images were removed and the
capture was fixed. They came from an over-collecting `a:blip` search that
pulled a second inline image off FIGURE paragraphs in two Acts
(excise-tariff-act-1921, corporate-law-economic-reform-program-act-1999);
`_figure_blobs` now takes only the first blob per FIGURE paragraph.


(f) DOCX footnotes, endnotes, headers and footers are not read
----------------------------------------------------------------

`docx_reader.iter_paragraphs` and `fidelity.docx_paragraphs` both consume
`word/document.xml` only. `word/footnotes.xml`, `word/endnotes.xml`,
`word/header*.xml` and `word/footer*.xml` are never read, so their text is
neither converted to AKN nor visible to the fidelity audit. Corpus impact
(measured 2026-09-07, P7): substantive footnotes in 6 Acts
(`crimes-(currency)-act-1981`, `cross-border-insolvency-act-2008`,
`international-fund-for-agricultural-development-act-1977`,
`marine-insurance-act-1909`, `national-environment-protection-council-act-1994`,
`psychotropic-substances-act-1976`), all inside Schedules that reproduce an
international instrument, 3 weakly operative and 3 explanatory; zero
substantive endnotes; zero operative header/footer content (all header/footer
text is `STYLEREF` running heads duplicating body headings, Word field-error
placeholders, or the OPC/FRL "electronic version... available on the Federal
Register of Legislation" publishing stamp). Accepted, not tracked as a
regression.
